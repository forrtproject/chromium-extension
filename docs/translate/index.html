FORRT ORE translations

Help translate the Open Research Extension

Each card shows one piece of text from the extension: the English original and a draft translation, most of them made by machine. Fix anything that is wrong or awkward, then press Approve (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter). Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back.

How to send us your review, and what the symbols mean
  1. Press Download my review. This saves a small .json file with only your changes and approvals.
  2. Send the file to the ORE team: attach it to a GitHub issue (drag it into the comment box) or email it to your FORRT contact.
  3. To continue on another computer, use Load a file with the file you downloaded.

Words in curly brackets such as {count} or {domain} are filled in by the extension; keep them exactly as they are. Text like {count, plural, one {# paper} other {# papers}} chooses a form by number: translate only the words inside the inner brackets, and keep # where the number goes. Keep tags such as <strong>…</strong> around the matching words. The preview under each card shows the result.

Don’t address users as “Sie” or “du”: use infinitives and impersonal phrasing (“Seite neu laden, um …”). Keep names such as FORRT, ORE, PubPeer and Retraction Watch unchanged.
