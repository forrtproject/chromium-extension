FORRT ORE translations

Help translate the Open Research Extension

ORE marks research papers on the web with replications, retractions and other evidence. Native-speaking researchers check how it reads in their language: no GitHub account needed. Pick your language to start.

All languages

Each card shows one piece of text from the extension: the English original and a machine-made draft. Fix anything that is wrong or awkward, then press Approve (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter). Drafts are sorted by how much attention they need: careful review first, then read it; routine quick checks can be skimmed and approved together. Your work is saved in this browser as you go; press Send my review when you're ready. No GitHub account needed.

Every contribution is acknowledged: reviewers are credited by name in ORE.

How to send us your review, and what the symbols mean
  1. Press Send my review and enter your name and email. Your name and a summary of your changes appear on issue #295; your email stays private and is only used to ask you about your review. You can send again later; only new changes are sent.
  2. No connection, or prefer email? Download saves a small .json file with your changes to send to your FORRT contact.
  3. To continue on another computer, use Load a file with a file you downloaded.

Words in curly brackets such as {count} or {domain} are filled in by the extension; keep them exactly as they are. Text that changes with a number or a setting is shown as separate sentences, one per case (for example “when {count} is 1” or “when {notice} is “concern””). Write each as a complete sentence; the line under each box shows how it will look. Keep tags such as <strong>…</strong> around the matching words. The preview under each card shows the result.

Keep names such as FORRT, ORE, PubPeer and Retraction Watch unchanged.

    Before you start

    Who should we credit? Your name appears in ORE's acknowledgements and next to your review on GitHub. You can change both later.

    Only the FORRT team sees your email, to credit you and to ask about your review; it is never shown publicly. Both are remembered in this browser only, so you don't have to type them again.

    Send my review

    Your name and a summary appear on issue #295. Your email is never shown: only the FORRT team can see it, to ask you about your review, and it is deleted after a year.

    We'll acknowledge your contribution and credit you by name in ORE, so use the name you'd like to be credited under.
