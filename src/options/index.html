FORRT ORE — Open Research Extension

Choose how ORE shows evidence on the papers you read. Email enables title-to-DOI matching and open-access lookups; other DOI-based checks work without it.
Why we ask for your email

Crossref, OpenAlex and Unpaywall ask for a contact address with each lookup. Without one, ORE can't look for open-access versions or match papers by title.

Your email is sent only as a contact parameter to those services and the NCBI identifier converter. Chrome sync stores it; FORRT never receives it. Clear the field and save to remove it.

Why these services ask for it

Crossref and OpenAlex place requests that include a contact address in their polite-access pool, which has faster rate limits and more reliable lookups. Unpaywall needs an address to answer open-access lookups.

Performance

Cache Storage Limit

How much lookup data ORE keeps on this computer
ORE keeps lookup results on this computer so repeat visits load faster. Minimum 10 MB; enter 0 for no limit. Your settings, the diagnostic log and the retraction list do not count towards it.

Update Reference Data

Retractions & Registered Reports
ORE checks papers against a local copy of the Retraction Watch database and the Registered Reports library. Both are refreshed weekly in the background; refresh them now, or every time the browser starts.

Troubleshooting

Debug Mode & Issue Reports

If something looks wrong, reproduce the problem, then report it. ORE adds its log of warnings and errors to the GitHub form, and you can read the log below before sending. Debug mode keeps a more detailed log.

Debug mode and log details
What the log contains

The log records what ORE looked up: DOIs found on the pages you visit, API outcomes and any errors. A report names only the site you report from; other pages appear as “page 1”, “page 2” and their links are removed. Entries older than seven days are deleted. The log stays on this computer and is never sent anywhere until you choose to open an issue with it — and nothing is submitted until you press GitHub's own button.

Your email address is never included. Read the report below before you share it — a GitHub issue is public.
