FORRT ORE — Open Research Extension

Choose how ORE shows evidence on the papers you read. Email enables title-to-DOI matching and open-access lookups; other DOI-based checks work without it.
Why we ask for your email

ORE queries the Crossref and OpenAlex APIs to resolve article DOIs. Both services ask for a contact email so they can place your requests in their polite-access pool — meaning faster rate limits and more reliable lookups while you browse.

Your email is sent as an API contact parameter to Crossref, OpenAlex, Unpaywall and the NCBI identifier converter. Chrome sync stores this setting; FORRT does not receive it. Clear the field and save to remove it.

Copy Citation

Citation Style

Every DOI pill has a Citation row that copies the reference in one click. Pick the style it uses here — that style, and only that style, is what the pill copies. The copy keeps its formatting, so italics survive a paste into Word or Google Docs; anywhere that takes plain text gets plain text. Citations are rendered by Crossref with doi.org as a fallback. Citations are fetched when you request one, including the sample preview below.

Performance

Cache Storage Limit

ORE stores lookup results locally so pages load faster on repeat visits. This shared limit covers all data providers; your settings, diagnostic logs and the retraction list are kept separately. Minimum 10 MB; enter 0 for unlimited.

Reference Data

Retractions & Registered Reports

ORE checks papers against a local copy of the Retraction Watch database and the Registered Reports library. Both are refreshed weekly in the background; refresh them now, or every time the browser starts.

Where ORE Runs

Sites by Category

ORE runs on journals, publishers, preprint servers and documents, and on any site not listed here. Shopping, video, messaging and web search start switched off. Untick a category to turn ORE off on all of its sites, or open it to choose site by site. Changes apply when a page is next loaded.

Blocked Domains

Site Blocklist

ORE runs on all websites by default. Add domains here to prevent it from scanning them.

PubPeer Comments

Muted Commenters

ORE shows PubPeer threads in its Meta Report panel. Add a commenter's display name — the name shown on their comment, such as Statcheck or a pseudonym like Anolis Jacare — to hide their comments from that panel. FORRT is muted by default because the panel already shows its data; remove it below if you'd rather see those comments too.

Troubleshooting

Debug Mode & Issue Reports

ORE always logs its warnings and errors. If something looks wrong, reproduce the problem and report the issue — the log is written straight into the GitHub form. Read it here first if you'd like to see what you're sending. Debug mode keeps a detailed log and adds a progress toast to each page so you can watch ORE work.
What the log contains

The log records what ORE looked up: DOIs found on the pages you visit, API outcomes and any errors. A report names only the site you report from; other pages appear as “page 1”, “page 2” and their links are removed. Entries older than seven days are deleted. The log stays on this computer and is never sent anywhere until you choose to open an issue with it — and nothing is submitted until you press GitHub's own button.

Your email address is never included. Read the report below before you share it — a GitHub issue is public.
